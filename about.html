Together we create, solve, design and construct!

Brilliant Onyx Contracting LLC is a premier construction and interior solutions provider based in Dubai, UAE. Since our establishment, we have been delivering exceptional design, build, and technical services across the Emirates.

Our comprehensive service portfolio includes building cleaning services, carpentry workshop operations, false ceiling and light partition installations, floor and wall tiling works, and complete interior decoration solutions. Operating from our headquarters in Dubai Silicon Oasis, we serve both commercial and residential clients throughout Dubai and surrounding areas.

We maintain high standards of quality in all our projects, from small-scale renovations to large commercial fit-outs. Our team of skilled professionals ensures that every project is executed with precision and attention to detail.
